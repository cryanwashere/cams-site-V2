The Laplacian is L = D − W. W marks the neighbor edges, and a pair is connected if either point lists the other among its k nearest. D holds each point’s degree.

The function f is 1 on category A and 0 on category B. Entry i of Lf is the sum over neighbors j of fi − fj, so |Lf|i counts the neighbors of point i in the other category. Swapping A and B gives the same colors.
