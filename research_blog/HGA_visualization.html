Jσ(R) = (1/mn) Σi,j exp( −arccos²⟨R p̂i, q̂j⟩ / 2σ² )

The sphere shows f(x) = (1/n) Σj exp( −d(x, q̂j)² / 2σ² ), so Jσ(R) is the average of f over the gold points.
